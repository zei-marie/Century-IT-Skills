# ₊˚⊹ About the Activity ✦

For **Activity 6**, I created a **one-page personal portfolio website** using an AI-assisted website builder.

The purpose of this activity was to explore website design, layout customization, digital content organization, and multimedia integration while creating a website that represents my personality, interests, and creativity.

As a nursing student, this activity also allowed me to explore technology outside the usual academic and healthcare setting and develop skills that can be useful in today's digital environment.

---

# ₊˚⊹ My Website ✦

### **"Through My Lens"**

I designed my website as a personal lifestyle and photography-inspired portfolio.

I wanted the website to feel:

- 🌷 Feminine
- ✦ Elegant
- 📸 Personal
- 🤎 Warm
- 🎀 Creative
- ✨ Visually organized

The overall design uses **soft pink, peach, cream, and warm brown tones** to create a soft and aesthetic appearance.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Website Features ✦

## 🏠 Header and Navigation

The website has a simple navigation bar with links to:

**Home • My Blog • About • Contact • Portfolio**

The navigation was designed to remain clean and minimal while allowing visitors to easily move through the website.

---

## 📸 Hero Section — "Through My Lens"

The hero section features three photographs arranged horizontally:

- A cozy aesthetic workspace
- My personal portrait
- A minimalist indoor plant

I used this section to immediately introduce the visual theme of my website and create a lifestyle-blog-inspired appearance.

---

## 🌷 About Me

The **More About Me!** section introduces me as a nursing student who enjoys:

- Creativity
- Technology
- Photography
- Discovering new experiences

I also included personal photographs arranged in a photo-collage style to make the website feel more personal.

---

## 🎀 Hobbies

The **Hobbies!** section contains a personal photograph and information about my interests and activities outside academics.

This section allows visitors to learn more about me beyond being a nursing student.

---

## ☕ Lifestyle Section

I also included an inspirational quote and a lifestyle photo collage to make the website more visually interesting.

These elements help communicate my personality and the things that inspire me.

---

## 💌 Contact Form

The website includes a contact form where visitors can leave a message.

This makes the portfolio more interactive and gives visitors a way to communicate with me.

---

# ₊˚⊹ My Creative Process ✦

I started by creating a detailed prompt describing the kind of website I wanted.

I specified the:

- 🎨 Color palette
- 🖼️ Layout
- 📸 Images
- ✍️ Typography
- 🌷 Overall aesthetic
- 📑 Website sections
- ✨ Interactive elements

I then used the AI-assisted website builder to generate the initial design.

After seeing the generated website, I reviewed the layout and made adjustments to make the website more aligned with my personal preferences.

---

# ₊˚⊹ AI-Assisted Design ✦

One of the most interesting parts of this activity was learning how AI can assist in website development.

Instead of starting completely from scratch, I was able to describe my ideas through a prompt and allow the AI-assisted website builder to translate those ideas into a website layout.

This helped me understand that creating a website can involve both **technology and creativity**.

# ₊˚⊹ What I Learned ✦

This activity taught me that website creation is not only about technical skills. It also involves **creativity, organization, visual communication, and attention to detail**.

I learned how different elements such as colors, typography, photographs, spacing, and layout work together to create a complete digital experience.

I also became more comfortable using AI-assisted tools and learned how important it is to give clear and specific instructions when working with AI.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Reflection ✦

Creating my personal portfolio website was a meaningful experience because it allowed me to express my personality and creativity through technology.

At first, I was not very familiar with website creation, so I relied on the instructions and explored the AI-assisted website builder step by step. As I continued working on the website, I became more comfortable experimenting with layouts, photographs, colors, and content.

I also realized that technology can be useful even outside my nursing studies. As a future healthcare professional, developing digital skills can help me adapt to a world where technology is becoming an important part of communication, education, and healthcare.

Most importantly, this activity taught me the importance of **patience, creativity, attention to detail, and continuous learning**. Creating a website is not simply about presenting information—it is also about communicating ideas and creating something that represents who you are.
