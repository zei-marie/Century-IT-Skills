# ₊˚⊹ Project Overview

For this activity, I created a personal branding design that represents my identity through the use of carefully selected colors, fonts, and visual elements.

The goal of this project was to understand how color palettes and typography influence the overall appearance and message of a design. Through this activity, I explored how design choices can create a specific mood, establish visual identity, and improve communication.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Design Concept

The concept of this design was inspired by simplicity, elegance, and personal expression.

I chose a minimalist style with warm and neutral tones to create a calm, professional, and approachable appearance. The design reflects a balance between creativity and professionalism, representing my personal identity as a student and future healthcare professional.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Color Palette

The selected color palette includes soft and warm tones such as light yellow, brown, red, pink, and beige.

## ✦ Light Yellow (#fffbd3)

This color represents warmth, positivity, and creativity. It provides a soft background that creates a welcoming and comfortable visual experience.

## ✦ Brown Shades (#3d1700, #641919)

Brown was used as the primary accent color because it represents stability, reliability, and elegance. It also complements the overall warm and cozy theme of the design.

## ✦ Crimson and Light Red (#b84c65, #fcc0c5)

These colors add a subtle emotional and personal touch to the design. They create contrast while maintaining a soft and balanced appearance.

## ✦ Light Orange (#e8d5b5)

This color supports the warm aesthetic of the design and helps create harmony between the different elements.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Typography

Typography was carefully selected to create a balance between creativity and readability.

## ✦ Display Font: Amsterdam Three

This script font was used for the main name and branding elements. Its elegant and handwritten appearance adds a personal and artistic touch to the design.

## ✦ Accent Font: Montserrat

Montserrat was used for supporting text because of its clean and modern appearance. It improves readability while maintaining a professional look.

## ✦ Body Font: Be Vietnam

Be Vietnam was selected for body information because it is simple, clear, and easy to read. It creates balance with the decorative elements of the design.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Design Principles Applied

## ✦ Consistency

Consistency was achieved by maintaining the same color palette, typography style, and overall visual theme throughout the designs.

## ✦ Contrast

Contrast was applied by combining dark brown elements with lighter backgrounds, allowing important details to stand out.

## ✦ Balance

The placement of text, spacing, and visual elements was arranged carefully to create a clean and harmonious composition.

## ✦ Simplicity

The design focused on essential elements only, preventing unnecessary details from distracting the viewer.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Creative Process

I started by identifying the style and message I wanted the design to represent.

After selecting the color palette, I explored different font combinations to find a balance between personality and readability. I adjusted the placement, spacing, and visual elements to create a cohesive and professional final output.

The process allowed me to understand how small design choices can influence how a person or brand is visually perceived.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that colors and typography play an important role in communicating emotions, identity, and meaning.

I realized that effective design requires careful consideration of every element because each choice contributes to the overall message of the output.

This activity improved my understanding of visual communication and helped me appreciate how design principles can be applied in different areas, including healthcare presentations, educational materials, and professional digital content.
