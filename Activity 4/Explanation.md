# GE 4120 – Midterm Examination
## Group 5 | Google Docs Presentation

### Overview

For our midterm examination in GE 4120, our group presented **Google Docs** through an interactive and engaging presentation. Instead of simply discussing the topic, we combined different activities such as **acting, interactive games, discussion, and a live demonstration** to make the presentation more enjoyable and easier to understand.

Our presentation covered the different features, purposes, and practical uses of Google Docs, including document creation, formatting, collaboration, comments, suggesting mode, version history, automatic saving, smart chips, citations, word count, and other useful functions. The presentation also emphasized how Google Docs can be used for collaboration and modern digital work.

## Our Presentation

We started our presentation with an **acting activity** to capture the attention of the class and introduce the topic in a more creative way.

After the introduction, we conducted an **interactive game** called **"Head, Shoulders, Knee and Cup"**. This allowed the class to participate before moving into the main discussion.

We then proceeded with our **Google Docs presentation**, where we explained its definition, purposes, features, and practical applications. We also discussed how Google Docs supports collaboration through different access levels such as **Viewer, Commenter, and Editor**.

The presentation also included important features such as:

- Creating and organizing documents
- Formatting text and paragraphs
- Using headings and document outlines
- Creating tables and inserting images
- Adding links and bookmarks
- Collaboration and sharing
- Comments and action items
- Suggesting mode
- Version history
- Automatic saving
- Smart chips and building blocks
- Citations and bibliography
- Word count and spelling/grammar checking
- Voice typing
- Exporting and downloading documents
- Privacy and sharing
- AI and Gemini in Google Docs

The presentation ended with a **live demonstration**, where we showed how some of the features of Google Docs work in an actual document. This helped connect the concepts we discussed with their practical application.

## Interactive Quiz

To assess the understanding of the whole class, we also created a **Google Forms quiz** based on the topics discussed during our presentation.

The quiz included questions about Google Docs features and functions, allowing our classmates to review what they learned and test their understanding.

## Reflection

This midterm examination helped us realize that presenting a topic does not always have to be limited to reading slides and explaining information. By including acting, games, discussion, and a demonstration, we were able to make the presentation more interactive and engaging for the class.

We also learned more about Google Docs while preparing for the presentation. We were able to understand not only its basic functions but also how features such as collaboration, comments, suggesting mode, version history, and automatic saving can be useful in school and future work.

Most importantly, the activity helped us improve our **communication, teamwork, creativity, and confidence in presenting in front of others**. It showed us that technology can make learning more interactive when it is combined with proper preparation and teamwork.

## Key Takeaway

> **Learning technology is not only about knowing the features—it is about knowing how to use them effectively, creatively, and collaboratively.**
