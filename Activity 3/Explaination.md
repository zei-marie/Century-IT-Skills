# ₊˚⊹ Project Overview

For this activity, I created an infographic titled **"Lost Records, Delayed Care: Secure Records, Safer Care!"**

The infographic highlights the challenges caused by missing, misplaced, or incomplete paper-based medical records and how these issues can affect the delivery of patient care.

The purpose of this project was to present a healthcare-related problem in a simple and engaging way while demonstrating how technology can provide solutions that improve efficiency, accessibility, and quality of healthcare services.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Concept and Purpose

The concept of the infographic was inspired by the importance of accurate and accessible patient information in healthcare settings.

Medical records contain essential details such as medical history, medications, allergies, laboratory results, and previous treatments. When these records are lost or delayed, healthcare professionals may experience difficulties in making timely decisions.

The infographic presents the problem of unreliable paper records and introduces digital medical records as a solution that allows healthcare workers to securely store, retrieve, and manage patient information.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Design Choices

## ✦ Visual Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy was applied by arranging the information into sections that guide the audience through the problem, effects, and proposed solution.

The title was emphasized first to immediately introduce the main issue, followed by the problem statements and the digital solution.

---

## ✦ Color Selection

The infographic uses blue, red, and light-colored backgrounds to create a healthcare-focused design.

Blue represents trust, security, and professionalism, while red highlights important healthcare concerns and draws attention to critical information.

The combination of these colors creates a balanced and recognizable medical theme.

---

## ✦ Typography

Different font styles were used to create contrast between headings and body text.

Bold fonts were used for important sections to capture attention, while simpler fonts were used for explanations to maintain readability.

The typography choices helped organize information and made the infographic easier to follow.

---

## ✦ Layout and Organization

The information was arranged in a step-by-step flow, starting with the healthcare problem, its effects, and the digital solution.

Icons and illustrations were incorporated to support the message and make complex information easier to understand.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Creative Process

I started by identifying a healthcare-related issue where technology could provide a meaningful solution.

After selecting the topic of medical record management, I organized the information into key sections to ensure that the message would be clear and understandable.

I then selected appropriate colors, illustrations, and layouts that matched the healthcare theme. The final design was revised to improve readability, visual balance, and overall communication effectiveness.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Mini Project Documentation

The proposed digital solution focuses on the use of **electronic medical records (EMR)** to improve the accessibility and security of patient information.

By storing records digitally, authorized healthcare workers can quickly retrieve important patient data, reduce documentation errors, and support faster clinical decision-making.

This technology-based approach can contribute to safer and more efficient healthcare delivery.
