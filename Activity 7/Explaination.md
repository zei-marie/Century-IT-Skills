# ₊˚⊹ About the Activity ✦

For **Activity 7**, I created a professional and organized resume using **digital tools and Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.

The goal of this activity was to learn how technology can help organize personal information, educational background, clinical experiences, nursing competencies, training, and qualifications into a clear and professional resume.

As a fourth-year nursing student, this activity also helped me prepare for future job applications while improving my digital literacy, creativity, and confidence in using technology.

---

# ₊˚⊹ My Resume ✦

I created my resume with a **clean, formal, and minimalist design**.

I chose a simple black-and-white layout with professional typography, balanced spacing, horizontal section dividers, and a formal portrait.

The design was kept simple so that the important information could be easily read and understood.

# ₊˚⊹ How I Created My Resume ✦

I started by preparing the information that I wanted to include in my resume.

I then used an **AI-assisted tool** to help organize the information and improve the wording of my qualifications and experiences.

I provided instructions about the desired layout, professional style, typography, spacing, and information that needed to be included.

After generating the initial output, I reviewed the content and made adjustments to ensure that the information accurately represented my education, skills, and experiences.

---

# ₊˚⊹ Using AI Responsibly ✦

One important lesson from this activity was that AI should be used as a **supporting tool**, not as a replacement for personal judgment.

AI helped me organize information and improve the presentation of my resume, but I still needed to check the details and personalize the content.

This helped me understand the importance of reviewing AI-generated information before using it for professional purposes.

---

# ₊˚⊹ What I Learned ✦

This activity taught me that creating a resume is more than simply listing achievements.

A good resume should present information in a way that is:

- ✦ Clear
- ✦ Organized
- ✦ Accurate
- ✦ Professional
- ✦ Relevant

I also learned how digital tools and AI can make the process of creating professional documents easier and more efficient.

# ₊˚⊹ Reflection ✦

Creating my resume using digital tools and Artificial Intelligence was a meaningful learning experience. I was able to explore different layouts, improve the wording of my qualifications, and organize my education, clinical experiences, training, and nursing competencies in a professional way.

This activity also gave me an opportunity to look back on my journey as a nursing student. While organizing my experiences, I realized how much I have learned and developed throughout my nursing education.
